Rubies Privacy Policy

Last updated November 18, 2026

In short: Rubies has no server and no accounts of its own. Your notes stay on your device and, if you turn on sync, in your own Google Drive. Nobody else, including the makers of Rubies, can see them.

What Rubies stores

Your sermon notes, handwriting, prayer list, Bible highlights, bookmarks and settings are saved in your browser's storage on your device (or on your computer, in the desktop app). They are not sent anywhere unless you turn on Google Drive sync.

Sync with Google Drive (optional)

If you choose Sign in with Google in Settings, Rubies asks Google for permission to:

  • Store its own data in your Google Drive (the drive.appdata permission). Rubies keeps one file there, in a hidden folder that only Rubies can open. It cannot see, change or delete any of your other Drive files.
  • See your name, email address and profile picture, so Settings can show which account you're signed in with.

Your device talks directly to Google. The data goes from your device to your Drive and back, and never passes through any server run by Rubies. Google's handling of your account is covered by Google's Privacy Policy.

Rubies uses information from Google only to save and sync your own notes for you. It is not shared, sold, used for advertising, or used to train AI models. Rubies' use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

What Rubies does not do

  • No analytics, tracking, cookies for tracking, or advertising.
  • No account with us, and nothing about you is collected by us.
  • The app itself is served by GitHub Pages, which, like any web host, may keep basic access logs (such as IP addresses) under GitHub's privacy statement.

Removing your data

  • Stop syncing: Settings → Sync across devices → Sign out. Your notes stay on the device.
  • Remove Rubies' access to your Google account and its file in Drive: open Google Drive settings → Manage apps → Rubies → Options → Delete hidden app data, then Disconnect from Drive. You can also remove access at myaccount.google.com/connections.
  • Remove notes from a device: uninstall the app, or clear this site's data in your browser settings.

Children

Rubies is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from them.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.

Contact
